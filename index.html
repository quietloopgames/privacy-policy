Privacy Policy for Number Collapse

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Number Collapse ("the App", "we", "our") handles information. We built this app to be simple and respectful of your privacy.

No Account Required

Number Collapse does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play. All game progress (level, streak, stars, coins, achievements) is stored locally on your device only, for the duration of your session. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and it resets if you close and reopen the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or share: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal identifying information. We do not require login via Google, Facebook, or any other service.

Advertising

Number Collapse may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) to support the free version of the app. If ads are shown, the advertising network may collect and process technical information (such as your advertising ID, device type, and general location) in accordance with its own privacy policy, in order to serve and measure ads. You can review Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. If you purchase the ad-free version, no advertising data is collected.

In-App Purchases

If the app offers optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads), these are processed entirely by the Google Play billing system. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Number Collapse does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not include chat, social features, or any way for users to communicate with each other.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features such as cloud save or leaderboards. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
