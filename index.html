Privacy Policy for Number Collapse

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Number Collapse is developed by Quiet Loop Games. This policy covers the iOS and Android versions. Available features may differ by platform and version.

Game progress and accounts

Progress, stars, coins, hints, themes, and unfinished puzzles are stored on your device to continue after reopening the app. You can play without connecting an account. Optional Apple Game Center and iCloud features on iOS may identify your player, sync progress, and show leaderboard scores. Optional Google Play Games on Android may identify your player and store and show the Total Stars leaderboard score. Android Play Games does not sync game saves.

Advertising and consent

Where ads are enabled, Google AdMob may display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads to non-Premium players. Google may process device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location derived from an IP address, and technical diagnostics for serving and measuring ads. Where required, the app requests advertising consent and provides privacy choices. Premium removes ads in the game. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Where available, Premium is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple's App Store on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android. The game checks purchase status to unlock and restore Premium. Apple or Google handles payment details; we do not receive your card or bank information. Pending purchases do not unlock Premium until completed.

Other information

Apple and Google services may process account identifiers, purchase status, device information, and technical diagnostics depending on which services you use. We do not ask for your contacts, photos, or precise location to play, and we do not sell personal information. The game has no chat or player-to-player messaging.

Your choices

You can use the game without connecting Game Center or Play Games. Deleting the app may remove local game data. Apple and Google manage data from their services under their own policies; you can manage connected accounts through device settings. Third-party services retain data under their own policies. You can contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game and service providers change. Contact quiteloopgames@gmail.com with questions.
